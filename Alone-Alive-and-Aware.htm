ALONE ALIVE AND AWARE

You are a husky. You have a Saiga‑12. The department store has decided its mannequins should be more alive.

WASD move · Shift sprint · Mouse aim · Click fire (semi‑auto, 8‑round box mag) · R reload · E howl (stuns & shoves) · Right‑click aim · F fullscreen · M mute
Earn points by hitting and breaking mannequins. Buy guns, ammo and meds at the glowing wall signs (E). 1–5 / Q / mouse wheel switch guns.
Streaks: break 5, 10 and 20 mannequins without getting hit to earn Treat Frenzy, Squeaky Strike and Zoomies. Press X to use one (20 s cooldown).
Walk to a door and press E to buy it and open a new room with better guns. The Mystery Box ($950) rolls a random gun.
Pick Survival, 7v7 Team Deathmatch, or P2P Team Deathmatch. P2P is a 1v1 duel over WebRTC. Choose HOST or JOIN and exchange the offer/answer codes; both players must connect before starting. No matchmaking server is included.
Controller: press Start. Sticks move/look · RT fire · LT aim · A buy · X reload · Y swap · B howl · LB streak.
Shells and bacon drop from broken mannequins.
