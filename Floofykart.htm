floofyKart

Kart racing where physics is merely a suggestion.

Arrows or WASD to drive · Shift to drift (release for a boost) · Space to use an item. The laws of physics get rewritten every few seconds. On a touchscreen gas is automatic: steer with the joystick (pull it down to brake), hold 🌀 while turning to drift, 🎁 uses an item. F toggles fullscreen.
