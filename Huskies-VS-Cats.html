Huskies vs. Cats

The cats are invading the doghouse! Collect falling bones, pick a husky card, then tap a snowy square to deploy it.

Keys 1–0 pick a card, X toggles the shovel, and the card tray scrolls (drag, swipe or wheel). As cats, tap the falling fish for energy (the huskies keep their own bones), and get 3 cats into the doghouse before 200 seconds pass, but don’t let your cat morale hit zero!
